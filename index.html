03About

I go where the festival crowd thins out — that's usually where the real story is standing quietly to one side.

By day I work in automotive market research; the rest of my time goes to a camera bag and a notebook. My work follows human emotion through cultural festivals and everyday ritual across India — Kashi's ghats, Gudi Padwa in Mumbai, the deserts of Rajasthan — shot, cut, and voiced myself.
